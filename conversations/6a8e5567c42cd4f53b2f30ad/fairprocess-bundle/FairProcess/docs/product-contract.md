# Product contract

## Purpose

FairProcess converts scattered procedural evidence into a reproducible case
timeline and a human-reviewed integrity report. Its first jurisdiction module
is Humboldt County code-enforcement recordation.

## Users

- Residents preserving and understanding their own records.
- Attorneys, advocates, and consultants reviewing multiple matters.
- Auditors and oversight bodies looking for repeatable procedural patterns.
- Agencies preventing incomplete or inconsistent actions before escalation.

## AI may

- classify documents;
- extract APNs, case numbers, parties, dates, instrument numbers, and citations;
- identify the exact page and text supporting an extracted fact;
- propose matches between case documents and recorder results;
- draft neutral follow-up correspondence; and
- explain a deterministic policy result in plain language.

## AI may not

- decide whether a violation occurred;
- infer criminal intent or conspiracy;
- impose a penalty, lien, abatement, transfer, or other governmental action;
- state that a record is missing when the available search is incomplete;
- send a communication without explicit human authorization; or
- alter an activated legal rule without review, versioning, and approval.

## First paid deliverable

A case audit package containing a document inventory, fact table, timeline,
recordation cross-check, unresolved questions, source citations, and reviewer
sign-off. The report describes procedural discrepancies; it does not make
unadjudicated accusations.
