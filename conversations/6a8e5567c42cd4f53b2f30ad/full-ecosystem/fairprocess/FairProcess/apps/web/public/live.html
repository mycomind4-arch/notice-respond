Paste raw municipal code text and AI will extract a draft policy bundle — recording deadlines, timing rules, service requirements. Generated rules are always drafts requiring legal review before activation.

FairProcess compares the selected policy rule, verified trigger dates, parcel identity, and supplied recorder results. A "not located" result is not proof that a record does not exist.
