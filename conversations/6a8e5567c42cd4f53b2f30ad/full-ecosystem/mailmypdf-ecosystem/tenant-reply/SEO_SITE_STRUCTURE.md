# Tenant Reply — Canonical SEO Site Structure

Preserve ecosystem-wide navigation. This is the SEO/content hierarchy only.

## Public
- `/` — Tenant Reply pillar: respond to landlords, property managers and housing notices; notice types; how it works; what to gather; AI handles heavy lifting; review; mailing/proof; featured workflows; FAQ; start.
- `/workflows` — Tenant Reply workflow hub: find your situation, search/discovery, category navigation, crawlable authority links.
- `/how-it-works` — upload notice/documents → AI understands → organize facts/evidence → build reply → review/approve → MailMyPDF sends → track/preserve proof.
- `/products`, `/pricing`, `/mail-a-pdf` — ecosystem/product pages.
- `/about`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/resources/*` — supporting SEO/footer content.
- `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/404` — legal/error.

## Workflow taxonomy
Eviction & possession; rent & fees; repairs & habitability; landlord/property disputes; deposit & move-out; tenant rights; general tenant responses.

## SEO flow
Pillar → workflow hub → specific authority page → authenticated reply workflow. Preserve existing workflow URLs and use intent-specific pages rather than thin keyword variants.

## Simplicity rule
AI handles notice/lease analysis, facts, deadlines, issues, evidence organization, tenant-rights analysis, strategy, drafting, validation, recipient checks and mailing preparation underneath a short user-facing flow.

## Navigation lock
No vertical-specific menu additions. Use the ecosystem shell.
