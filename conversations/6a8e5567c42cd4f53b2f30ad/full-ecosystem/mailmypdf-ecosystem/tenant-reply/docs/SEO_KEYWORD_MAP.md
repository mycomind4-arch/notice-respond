# Tenant Reply SEO Keyword Map

Research basis: Keyword.com US English keyword discovery, August 2026.

## Strategy

Tenant Reply should target **tenant problem + action** intent rather than only broad tenant-rights education. Broad tenant-rights searches are large, but conversion is strongest around notices, disputes, deposits, repairs, and eviction.

## Priority clusters

### Core tenant intent
- tenant rights — 6,600/mo
- renters rights — 2,900/mo
- landlord tenant law — 2,900/mo
- tenant legal help — 1,300/mo
- tenant rights group — 1,300/mo

### Notices / eviction
- squatters eviction notice — 1,600/mo
- laws on eviction — 1,000/mo
- tenant eviction rights — 320/mo
- renters rights eviction — 320/mo
- eviction rights — 90/mo

### Disputes / property problems
- landlord and tenant disputes — 590/mo
- apartment flooded what are my rights — 260/mo
- tenants rights when landlord sells property — 1,000/mo
- renters deposit laws — 170/mo
- no lease tenant rights — 170/mo
- tenant privacy rights — 140/mo

## Recommended architecture

Tenant Reply
├── Respond to landlord notice
├── Eviction / termination notice
├── Security deposit dispute
├── Repairs / habitability
├── Rent dispute
├── Lease / no-lease issues
├── Privacy / access issues
└── Move-out / property-sale correspondence

## Rule

Use broad rights content to attract searchers, but convert them into document-based workflows: upload notice/lease, extract facts and dates, organize evidence, draft a factual response, and preserve mailing proof. Jurisdiction-specific rules must be authoritative and versioned.
