# GovReply — SEO Content Audit

## Verdict
**Keep the focused product slice; rebuild acquisition around the government-document situations people actually search.**

The current thesis is strong: receive → understand → deadline → evidence → strategy → response → review → proof, with source-grounded analysis and explicit uncertainty. fileciteturn50file0

## Primary search intent

- how to respond to a government letter
- government notice response
- respond to an agency letter
- what to do after receiving a government notice
- government correspondence response letter
- government deadline response
- upload government notice and understand it

## Content architecture

- `/respond-to-a-government-notice`
- `/understand-a-government-letter`
- `/respond-to-an-agency-letter`
- `/government-notice-response-letter`
- `/government-letter-deadline`
- `/prepare-a-government-response`
- `/prove-government-response-was-sent`

GovReply should own the **understand/analyze/respond** intent while Notice Respond and Code Enforcement own narrower situations. MailMyPDF remains the delivery/proof layer.

## Conversion

Primary CTA: **Upload your government letter**.

The first screen should answer: What is this? What does it ask me to do? Is there a stated deadline? What information is missing? What are my next options?

## Safeguards

Never invent deadlines, statutes, rights, or compliance conclusions. Every material extraction should retain document provenance, consistent with the current product architecture. fileciteturn50file0
